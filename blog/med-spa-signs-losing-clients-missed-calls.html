7 signs your med spa is losing clients to missed calls (and how to fix it)

July 5, 2026 · 7 min read · Sol Reception

Most med spa owners have no idea how much money leaks out through the phone every month. The bookings you never see do not show up on a report. They just quietly go to the spa down the street that happened to pick up.

If any of the seven signs below sound familiar, your practice is likely losing clients to missed calls, and the fix is simpler than you think.

The stakes are high because an aesthetics client is worth a lot. The average med spa client is worth more than $7,800 in lifetime value, so even a handful of missed calls a week can add up to six figures a year. Here is how to tell if it is happening to you.

01

Your phone rings during treatments and nobody can pick up

If your estheticians and injectors are the same people expected to answer the phone, you are missing calls every single day. Salons and med spas miss up to 35% of incoming calls because staff physically cannot stop mid-treatment to grab the phone. You would not walk out on a client mid-injection to answer a ring, and your team knows it, so the call goes to voicemail.

The problem is that the busiest treatment hours are also the busiest call hours. One study of 22 practices found 42% of calls, nearly 3,000 out of 7,000, went unanswered for exactly this reason. Every one of those was someone who wanted to give you money. Picture a Friday afternoon with every room full: three people call in the same ten minutes asking about your most popular treatment, and not one of them gets a human. By Monday they have booked elsewhere.

02

Most of your inquiries come in after you have closed

People research treatments at night. They finish work, put the kids to bed, and finally have a quiet moment to think about that Botox appointment or the consultation they have been meaning to book. Then they call, get voicemail, and move on to the next spa that can help them right away.

After-hours interest is real demand, not junk. Businesses that answer or text back those inquiries recover 30 to 40% of them. For a spa getting even 10 after-hours calls a week, that is three or four consultations a week you are currently handing to competitors while you sleep.

03

Your voicemail is full of hang-ups, not messages

Here is a myth worth killing: people who reach voicemail leave a message and wait for a callback. They do not. Up to 85% of callers who do not get through never call back at all, and 52% of spa customers abandon the call after just three minutes on hold.

So an empty voicemail box is not good news. It means callers are hanging up and searching again, not patiently waiting for you. If your team feels good because “nobody left a message,” that is the sound of bookings leaving.

04

Your ad spend is climbing but new bookings are flat

If you are pouring money into Instagram ads, Google, and promotions but your new client numbers are not moving, look at the phone before you blame the marketing. You may be generating plenty of interest and dropping it at the last step.

Med spas lose up to 30% of potential bookings to missed calls. That means for every ten new clients your marketing earns, three can disappear at the moment they try to reach you. You are paying full price to make the phone ring, then letting a third of those rings go unanswered. The leak is not the top of the funnel. It is the front desk.

05

Clients tell you they “booked somewhere else first”

When a potential client cannot reach you, they do not wait. They call the next spa on their list, and whoever answers first usually wins the booking. Aesthetics shoppers almost always contact more than one provider, and the first to respond gets the appointment.

If you are hearing “I actually already booked with someone” during callbacks, that is not bad luck. That is speed losing you the client. You were probably their first choice. You just were not the first to pick up.

06

Your front desk is drowning during peak hours

When every chair is full and the lobby is busy, your front desk is trying to check clients in, take payments, answer questions, and field a ringing phone all at once. Something has to give, and it is almost always the phone. Each unanswered call during peak hours is usually someone trying to book the exact slots that are filling up fastest.

One overwhelmed person cannot hold five conversations at the same time. So the callers stack up, get voicemail, and the highest-intent bookings of your day slip away while your team is heads-down serving the clients already in front of them. The cruel part is that these are your warmest leads. A person calling during your busiest hour is ready to book now, and they are the easiest client you will lose all week.

07

You cannot say how many calls you actually miss

This is the quietest sign and the most dangerous. If someone asked how many calls your spa missed last week, could you answer? Most owners cannot, because a missed call leaves no trace. There is no line item for the consultation you never booked.

Three missed calls a day, at a $600 average booking and a 30% booking rate, is more than $130,000 in lost revenue a year. The number is invisible, which is exactly why it goes unfixed year after year while owners assume business is just slow.
